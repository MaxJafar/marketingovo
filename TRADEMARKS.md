The source code license and this trademark policy are separate. Apache-2.0
grants broad rights to the code and explicitly reserves trademark rights: it
does not grant rights to the AGENTseo, GolemWorkers, or Golem Workers names,
logos, product icons, slogans, domains, or trade dress (collectively, the
"Golem marks"). Because the code is now freely redistributable, this policy is
the primary protection for the project's identity.

## Allowed without separate permission

- Truthfully state that unmodified software is compatible with, based on, or a
  fork of AGENTseo Community Edition.
- Link to the official project and accurately identify its license.
- Use the word mark in factual editorial coverage, reviews, and tutorials.

## Permission required

- Naming a fork, service, package, domain, company, event, or product with a
  Golem mark or a confusingly similar name.
- Using Golem artwork, icons, or trade dress in a way that suggests an official
  release, endorsement, certification, or partnership.
- Representing a modified build as "AGENTseo" without a prominent statement
  that it is modified and not maintained by GolemWorkers.

Forks must choose distinct branding, preserve license and copyright notices,
and say where they differ from the official distribution. You may use phrasing
such as "based on AGENTseo Community Edition" in a secondary description.

To request trademark permission, contact `legal@golemworkers.com`. Permission
may be conditioned or withdrawn to protect users from confusion. This policy is
not legal advice and will be reviewed before the first public release.
