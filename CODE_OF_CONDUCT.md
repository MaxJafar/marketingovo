# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

Expected behavior includes constructive technical disagreement, empathy toward
users, clear disclosure of conflicts, respect for privacy, and feedback aimed at
the work rather than the person. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
doxxing, sexualized conduct, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban a
participant when needed to protect the community. Report conduct concerns
privately by opening a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/MaxJafar/marketingovo/security/advisories/new),
which is visible only to the maintainer, or by contacting the maintainer
directly through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially and
good-faith reporters will not face retaliation.
