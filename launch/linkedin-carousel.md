# Eight-slide LinkedIn carousel

Format: 1080×1350, one idea per slide, high-contrast text, and no customer data in screenshots.

**Headline**

**Subhead**

Which five deserve attention this week?

**Footer**

AGENTseo `0.11` alpha

**Visual direction**

A dense issue list fades behind five clear action cards.

**Alt text**

“A long SEO issue list narrowing into five prioritized actions.”

## Slide 2 — An issue is not a decision

**Headline**

Severity alone is not enough.

**Body**

A useful action also needs reach, exposure, confidence, effort, affected URLs, evidence, and a verification step.

**Visual direction**

One action card with labeled fields; keep values illustrative and mark the screenshot as demo data.

**Alt text**

“An SEO action card with impact, effort, confidence, affected URLs, evidence, and verification fields.”

## Slide 3 — Bring the evidence together

**Headline**

One action queue. Multiple sources.

**Body**

Crawl, search performance, analytics, page performance, trends, and SERP providers can contribute evidence through normalized contracts.

**Footer**

Connected does not automatically mean fresh.

**Visual direction**

Source tiles flow into one evidence record. Show available, stale, and unavailable states.

**Alt text**

“Several SEO and analytics sources feeding a normalized evidence record with explicit source states.”

## Slide 4 — Make priority explainable

**Headline**

No mystery score.

**Body**

`priority-v1` combines severity, organic exposure, conversion exposure, URL reach, confidence, and an effort multiplier.

**Footer**

The formula is documented. Missing inputs lower confidence.

**Visual direction**

Show the weighted formula beside a 0–100 result. Avoid implying predictive revenue.

**Alt text**

“The documented priority-v1 weighted formula producing a transparent action score.”

## Slide 5 — Missing is not zero

**Headline**

**Body**

**Visual direction**

Compare “0 clicks” with “GSC unavailable”; highlight why they are different claims.

**Alt text**

“A comparison between a measured zero and unavailable Search Console data.”
