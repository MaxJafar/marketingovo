# AGENTseo on Golem Workers

AGENTseo Community Edition is the local-first, single-user product in this
repository. AGENTseo on Golem Workers is a separate proprietary service at
[golemworkers.com/seo](https://golemworkers.com/seo).

The commercial edition provides infrastructure and collaboration rather than
artificially disabling the local analysis engine:

- always-on hosted crawl and monitoring workers;
- organizations, RBAC, shared projects, and audit logs;
- managed credentials, provider credits, and centralized billing;
- portfolio and agency views, white-label share links, and hosted artifacts;
- large-scale rank, backlink, competitor, and scheduled research;
- approval-gated AI action plans, briefs, developer tickets, and execution;
- GitHub, Jira, Linear, CMS, alerting, and report-delivery workflows;
- retention controls, cross-project benchmarks, SLA, and enterprise support.

Commercial code and entitlements are not included here as dormant or
downloadable implementations. Community exports omit every secret and can be
imported into Golem Workers after integrations are reconnected.
